How do Zeroto1 Academy lessons work?

  • Hook Grab attention with a fun, surprising fact.
  • Video Watch approved, kid-friendly videos.
  • Read Learn more with guided reading.
  • Activity Reinforce learning with a hands-on activity.
  • Practice Check understanding with a short quiz.
  • Deep Dive Explore more with AI-generated follow-up lessons.
