Light Travels in Straight Lines

💡 Big question

How does light move from a source?

A flashlight beam makes a straight path through mist because tiny droplets scatter some of the light toward your eyes. Follow that path and you will see why shadows have sharp edges and why you cannot peek around a corner without a mirror.

🎯 By the end

You will be able to say, in your own words, that light travels in straight lines and point to clues (beams, shadows, blocked views) that show it.
