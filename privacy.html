Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

zeroto1.academy ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of our users and their families. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — the parent or guardian's name, email address, and password when creating a household account.
  • Billing information — payment details are processed securely by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage information — lessons started and completed, quiz results, practice progress, and features used.
  • Lesson content — questions a child types to generate a custom lesson.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the service.
  • To personalize lessons and recommend follow-up content.
  • To show parents a view of finished work and where a child got stuck.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To communicate important service updates.

3. Children's privacy

zeroto1.academy is designed for children. Accounts are created and managed by a parent or guardian. We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide the service and apply safety guardrails to all generated lesson content. We do not knowingly collect more information from children than is necessary, and we do not use children's information for targeted advertising.

4. Information sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate the service (for example, payment processing and hosting), under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your rights

You may access, update, or delete your account information at any time by contacting us at hello@zeroto1.academy. Depending on your region (including under GDPR for European users and India's DPDP Act), you may have additional rights regarding your personal data.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email hello@zeroto1.academy.
