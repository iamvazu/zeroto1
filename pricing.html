FAQ

Do I pay for each child?
No. One price covers your whole household.
What is included?
Lessons, math practice, rewards, Deep Dive, and a parent view of finished work and where a child got stuck. If a child asks a question and it becomes a lesson, that counts toward about 75 custom lessons every 30 days.
What happens when we use the 75 custom lessons?
They reset every 30 days. Unused ones do not carry over. If you need more, you can purchase more custom lessons.
Can I cancel?
Yes. You keep access until the time you already paid for ends.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. Within 30 days of paying, email hello@zeroto1.academy and we will refund you. The charge on your card says ZEROTO1.
